## What the design keeps from the literature

1. **Matching identifiers against a published term list is MWE identification, and its published profile is this library's doctrine.** [Constant et al. 2017](https://aclanthology.org/J17-4005.pdf) define identification as annotating tokens "by associating them with known MWEs (types)". In [PARSEME edition 1.2](https://aclanthology.org/2020.mwe-1.14.pdf) the one pure lexicon matcher, Seen2Seen, posts the best MWE-based precision of any system in either track — 76.2 macro-averaged — with recall 0.6 on expressions outside its lexicon, and per-language best F1 tracks the unseen ratio at Pearson −0.90. Coverage of the lexicon is what moves identification, and the matcher's own job is precision on stated entries with few or no matches elsewhere.
2. **The two error modes that transfer to identifiers are literal use and permissive normalisation.** Constant et al. record that longest-match lookup "tends to overgenerate", through lemma-level matching that admits variants an entry forbids, and through literal uses of listed expressions. The other documented modes — inflection, discontiguity, coordination overlap — cannot arise inside an identifier, which makes every identifier match what [Sag et al. 2002](https://verbs.colorado.edu/~mpalmer/Ling7800/SagMWE.pdf) call a fixed expression, for which "a simple words-with-spaces representation is sufficient". The normalisation normalisation `NormalisedTerms` already exposes is where that risk enters, and part 3b's null is the guard.
3. **Domain terminology is mostly multiword.** Sag et al.: 41% of WordNet 1.7 entries are multiword, "almost certainly an underestimate", and specialist terminology "overwhelmingly consists of MWEs". The 10–100× separation the sweep measured is the expected shape of term vocabularies, and the reason phrase evidence deserves a route into the arithmetic.
4. **Partition, then count; one subject per phrase; the word is the fallback and fallback mass is never duplicated.** [ToPMine](http://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol8/p305-ElKishky.pdf) constrains all words of a phrase to one topic and partitions each document into non-overlapping units; [PDLDA](https://aclanthology.org/D12-1020/) derives the same from non-compositionality; [SegPhrase](http://hanj.cs.illinois.edu/pdf/sigmod15_jliu.pdf) calls unsegmented raw frequency "a critical bottleneck" and counts each token occurrence inside exactly one phrase instance. Katz backoff (via [Goodman](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0108005)) is the arithmetic for the fallback: the higher-order unit is used where it has a count, and the lower order receives exactly the discounted mass. `CollocatedWords`' longest-match no-overlap walk is the landed form of all of this; the design extends its index and never its arithmetic.
5. **A written multiword label nearly fixes the sense of its words.** [Finlayson & Kulkarni 2011](https://aclanthology.org/W11-0805.pdf): WordNet 1.6 multiword lemmas average polysemy 1.07 against 1.53 for single words — *world* has nine senses, *record* fourteen, *world record* one — and replacing constituents with a detected MWE is worth +3.3 to +6.1 F1 on most-frequent-sense disambiguation, nearly all of it from plain longest-match detection. [Yarowsky 1993](https://aclanthology.org/H93-1052.pdf): an adjacent content word pins a binary sense at .96–.97 mean precision, and a single unopposed observation already classifies above 92%. [Gale, Church & Yarowsky 1992](https://aclanthology.org/H92-1045.pdf) and [Yarowsky 1995](https://aclanthology.org/P95-1026.pdf): propagating one such anchor across a discourse measures 98–99.8% accurate, applied probabilistically with counted evidence — at two occurrences it reclassifies little, at four it overrides. This is part 2's mechanism and the derivation of its weight.
6. **A chance bar for phrase evidence needs no phrase-frequency reference.** ToPMine decides phrasehood against the corpus's own independence expectation, μ₀ = L·p(P₁)·p(P₂), and the shuffled-text surrogate ([Montemurro & Zanette 2011](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0019875)) generalises this: permute the corpus's own tokens and recompute the adjacency statistic, so the corpus's unigram margins are the reference. Document-level permutation is the form already in print for keyness ([Mildenberger 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.13383)) and already in this tree as `PermutationNull`, with the [Phipson & Smyth](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21044043/) estimator. The permutation p is bounded in [1/(n+1), 1] by its definition. This settles the commission's open question: part 3b's bar is a permutation of the phrases the repository itself wrote.
7. **The nested-term correction needs only the matches themselves.** C-value ([Frantzi, Ananiadou & Mima 2000](https://personalpages.manchester.ac.uk/staff/sophia.ananiadou/IJODL2000.pdf), formula verified through [Marciniak & Mykowiecka 2014](https://aclanthology.org/W14-4805.pdf)) subtracts from a matched term's count the mean count of the longer matched terms containing it; a term seen only inside one longer match scores 0. The inputs are frequencies, lengths and containment — no stop list, no reference corpus. `TermSpans`' longest-at-each-position walk already implements the partition this correction assumes. The log₂-length factor beside it is precedent with a stated rationale and a chosen form, patched twice in its own literature; it is not adopted.
8. **Refused, with reasons.** Raw PMI: no derived bound — its maximum, −log₂ P(x,y), moves with the pair's rarity ([Church & Hanks 1990](https://aclanthology.org/J90-1003.pdf); [Bouma 2009](https://svn.spraakdata.gu.se/repos/gerlof/pub/www/Docs/npmi-pfd.pdf)). Log-likelihood ratio: 2N times a mutual information, which is a KL divergence ([Dunning 1993](https://aclanthology.org/J93-1003.pdf); Bouma). Weirdness: unbounded by its own table, with a hand-chosen threshold and undocumented handling of absent words ([Ahmad et al., TREC-8](https://trec.nist.gov/pubs/trec8/papers/surrey2.pdf)). NC-value: a chosen 0.8/0.2 split. Supervised MWE identifiers: PARSEME's own unseen collapse. If an effect size beyond a share is ever needed for a phrase, the bounded candidates are NPMI in [−1, 1] and logDice ≤ 14, both derived and the second needing no corpus size ([Rychlý 2008](https://www.fi.muni.cz/usr/sojka/download/raslan2008/13.pdf)); none is needed below.

## Part 2 — a written label gives its sense a counted weight

Within a subject scheme's arm, every sense is uncounted and weighs 0.5. The rule this part adds:

**A sense whose placing label the repository wrote as one run of adjacent words takes the run's occurrence count as its `uses`. Every other sense keeps the uncounted 0.5.**

| Piece | What it is | What it cites |
|---|---|---|
| The matcher | the landed span walk, at the WORDS normalisation only | `TermSpans`; the WORDS restriction because lemma-level admission is the documented overgeneration route (Constant et al. 2017) |
| The evidence | multi-word matches already recorded in `matches` for schemes in the term path; the same walk over arXiv and OpenAlex labels for the arms the term path does not cover | `evidence.json`; `NormalisedTerms` |
| The weight | the count of times the repository wrote the full label | a tagged corpus counting occurrences — the same frame as WordNet's SemCor counts, with the repository as the corpus; single unopposed sightings already carry (Yarowsky 1993 §6.4) |
| The scope | the repository | one sense per discourse (Gale, Church & Yarowsky 1992); at one or two writings the count competes modestly with 0.5 rivals, which is Yarowsky 1995's own caution arriving through the arithmetic |
| The bound | shares in [0, 1] | `DomainMasses.sharesOf` normalises over the word's senses, unchanged |

Counting frames never mix. WordNet's arm keeps SemCor's counts, and the repository's writing reaches it only through the existing discourse factor bounded in [1, 2]. The corroboration count applies only within arms whose senses are all uncounted, where it competes only with 0.5.

Worked example, at the sweep's figures: strata writes `interest rate` as adjacent words (FpML's concept, 72 distinct multi-word matches on strata). The sense placing `rate` under the scheme's interest-rate area takes `uses` 72. Against five uncorroborated rival senses at 0.5 each, `rate`'s share for that area moves from 1/6 to 72/74.5 ≈ 0.97 — and a scheme whose label the repository never writes is untouched.

**The order of work is `AGREED_SENSES.md`'s.** Step 1 runs against the committed evidence of the nine members and reads no corpus: apply the rule to the committed `matches` and venn inputs, print each affected word's placement before and after. **Step 1 passes when**, on strata, `rate`, `leg`, `strike` and `tenor` move toward the areas whose labels strata writes, in the arms that state those labels, and no abstention changes — corroboration adds weight only where labels already exist. Then the pages regenerate. If the rule later enters any reading that feeds `reading.json`, it takes the full backtest first: **kept when** no member's leader moves further from the area its manifest states and the finance members' finance-adjacent mass concentrates; **refuted by** concentration that rises only because coverage fell, or finance members that do not move — they are the members the rule was measured on, so an unmoved finance member means the mechanism does nothing where its evidence is densest.

## Part 3 — the phrase as a pooled unit, and its chance bar

### 3b, first: the term path qualifies against a permutation

The sweep judged FIBO-on-fineract against hand-picked controls. The derived bar permutes one of the two sides 999 times, recounts, and qualifies the observed count where it exceeds the chance-expected maximum at the 1/(n+1) quantile, exactly as `ChanceExpectedBest` already computes; the bound [1/1000, 1] follows from the estimator's definition. **Which side to permute was the open question, and the run settled it against the side this design first named.** The design as written permuted the repository — its own words across its declared names, each name keeping its length — on the reading that the repository's unigram margins are the reference the literature's independence expectation asks for. That was measured and refuted twice over, and what landed permutes the vocabulary instead: its own words across its own terms, each term keeping its length, so the reference states as many terms of the same lengths from the same word list and differs only in which words the publisher put beside which. "Where the work stands" below records both runs and why the second reference is the one that answers the question. Neither needs a phrase-frequency resource.

**Done when**, on the sweep's members, the field vocabularies clear their bar on their fields and the same vocabularies on the controls do not, and BIAN continues to fail both ways. **Refuted by** controls clearing the bar at rates comparable to members — that would mean the count measures vocabulary size rather than subject matter, and the null's conditioning must be re-examined before any phrase figure is reported at all.

### 3a, last: the published term as the pooled unit

`PublishedPhrases` gains a second source: the multi-word concept labels of the bundled term vocabularies, which `TermIndex` already states in identifier form. `CollocatedWords`' walk is unchanged. The reference corpus is re-pooled in the same change — the clones are local, `CorpusPoolCommand` regenerates the tables, and the unit changes on both sides of the ranking at once, which is the one symmetric move the corpus rules allow.

In the topic distribution, a merged run the topical dictionaries do not label is seen, counted in the denominator, and votes nothing. That removes `rate` read as metrology and `leg` read as anatomy from every finance name that writes the full term — the +3.3 to +6.1 F1 shape Finlayson & Kulkarni measured for exactly this replacement — at the price of removing every vote those words cast. Whether the price buys placement accuracy is not arguable from here; it is the backtest question.

**Criteria, stated before the run.** On the nine members: the vocabulary-ranking verdicts churn no more than the one-in-ten resample baseline, compared as diff sets, never totals; no member's subject placement falls below its own null; the finance members' placements do not degrade. On the controls: no control gains matched-term units at member-like rates — the literal-use guard, and 3b's bar applied to the units 3a creates. **Refuted by** overgeneration on the controls or degraded placements on members whose words currently vote correctly; a refutation removes the taxonomy source from `PublishedPhrases` and leaves 3b standing, since the bar is worth having for evidence that already exists.

## What this design does not do

- It bundles no phrase-frequency reference. The Leipzig collection's neighbour-co-occurrence tables are the one candidate found, and its current licence and contents could not be verified this round; if ever bundled, the counts would be the admissible part and never the log-likelihood scores beside them.
- It adopts no phrase effect-size statistic. The share and the permutation p carry the reporting; NPMI and logDice are recorded as the bounded candidates should one ever be needed.
- It leaves step 7's pooled unit alone until 3a's backtest reports.

An arXiv subject is described in 29 units on average, and the runs the vocabularies absorb there are its most specific ones: 40 distinct runs over 53 occurrences, led by `machine_learning` ×7, and including `natural_language_processing`, `programming_languages`, `formal_languages`, `speaker_recognition`, `information_extraction`, `program_verification`, `distributed_computing` and `social_networks`. A subject whose one discriminating phrase becomes a unit that votes nothing stops being reachable, so every repository sits further from every subject.

  **What the refutation leaves standing, and what it names next.** 3b stands, as the plan said it would. The unit change is right for the term path and wrong for the arithmetic that reads prose, so the two cannot share one index while a merged run votes nothing. **The measurement that would settle the repair: give the merged run the vote its own concept carries** — `ConceptTopics` already places a matched concept — and re-run these 36 levels. A run that arrives with a subject is additive where one that arrives silent is subtractive, and that is the difference between the two columns above. Failing that, the narrower move is two indexes: the vocabularies' runs on the repository side of the comparison and the dictionaries' alone on the published-subject side, which is a measurement rather than an argument.
